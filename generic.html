More about us

Studying Hipposideridae reproduction and dimorphism

Prof. Alice Hughes Lab - University of Melbourne

Ahna Ripper-Wilson

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Melbourne, Australia. My research focuses on understanding and characterising the multifaceted aspects of reproduction and dimorphism of Hipposideridae bats. Population persistence lies at the heart of conservation, and persistence is impossible without effective reproduction. I believe developing a deep understanding of reproduction is therefore fundamental to conservation management, underpinning environmental protection, in-situ conservation, and interventional breeding efforts.

My earlier focus centered on native Australian marsupials, however, I became increasingly drawn to bat reproduction, an area that remains comparatively understudied despite its remarkable diversity!

If you're interested in collaborating - send me an email and I’ll be excited to discuss how we could work together!
