Hipposideridae Reproductive Repository

This project aims to develop a comprehensive reproductive repository for Hipposideridae bats,
incorporating specimen records alongside standardised photographic and imaging data
of reproductive organs and scent glands

Repository Value & Applications

By compiling these datasets across multiple species and geographic regions, we seek to improve the
assessment of reproductive status, identify morphological cues associated with different reproductive
stages, and better understand the timing and seasonality of reproductive events. Ultimately, the database
will provide a valuable resource for researchers investigating reproductive biology, life-history strategies,
and the conservation of Hipposideridae populations.

Photographs

Photographs, including date, location, and a scale bar where possible, of:
• Full body
• Scent glands
• Nose-leaf morphology (front, lateral, dorsal, and ventral views)
• External genitalia
• Pubic nipples in all females and males (if present)
• Thoracic nipples in females, including non-lactating individuals
Note* Photographs and imaging of museum specimens are also encouraged

Reproductive and Ecological Data

Including but not limited to:
• Date and geographic location of capture or observation
• Females displaying evidence of lactation
• Records of males with scrotal testes
• Females with visible or palpable pregnancy

Behavioural and Roost Observations

Including but not limited to:
• Qualitative or anecdotal observations of maternity roosts
• Behavioural interactions within roosts
• Observations of pups and post-natal development
• Roost environmental conditions, including humidity and temperature

Collaboration & Acknowledgements

We view this project as a collaborative effort and greatly value the contributions of participating researchers.

  • How to add your data to the repository

    Upload your data to the repository through the provided 'contribute' button which will direct you to a google form. Data should be uploaded as ZIP files titled 'Species_Data-Type', including dates, locality, and a document with a clear format on how to acknowledge and reference your contributions. If you have large amounts of data, you can also choose to submit labeled ZIP files to a gdrive here.

  • Project Goals

    The goal of this project is to create a comprehensive reproductive repository for Hipposideridae bats. This repository will be used to create resources for researchers investigating reproductive biology, life-history strategies, and the conservaton of Hipposideridae bats. Including a manual for the assessment of reproductive status in Hipposideridae bats.

  • Contribution acknowledgements

    Individuals who make substantive contributions to the development of the repository, including the provision of data, expertise, or ongoing collaboration, will be invited to co-author a data paper describing the repository and its applications, which will form part of my PhD research. Contributors will also be appropriately acknowledged in any resulting publications, presentations, and project outputs.

  • Project Contacts

    For any enquiries or further information, please contact Ahna Ripper-Wilson and Prof. Alice Hughes using the email address provided at the bottom of this page. We welcome enquiries from researchers, museums and collections staff, field researchers, conservation organisations, and other individuals or institutions with relevant knowledge, specimens, datasets, or expertise that may contribute to the project.
